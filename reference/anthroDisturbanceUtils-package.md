# anthroDisturbanceUtils: Utilities for the Anthropogenic Disturbance 'SpaDES' Modules

Helpers shared by the anthropogenic disturbance 'SpaDES' modules
('anthroDisturbance_DataPrep', 'anthroDisturbance_Generator' and
'potentialResources'): writing a list of disturbance layers to disk as
an indexed archive, and reading it back as 'terra' vector layers;
combining, cleaning and replacing the layers of such a list; and mapping
where any disturbance is present.

## Modules

The modules that use this package are on GitHub, in two versions:

- Northwest Territories, by Tati Micheletti:

  - <https://github.com/tati-micheletti/anthroDisturbance_DataPrep>

  - <https://github.com/tati-micheletti/anthroDisturbance_Generator>

  - <https://github.com/tati-micheletti/potentialResourcesNT_DataPrep>

- Yukon, by Alex M. Chubaty (FOR-CAST), forked from the above:

  - <https://github.com/FOR-CAST/anthroDisturbance_DataPrep>

  - <https://github.com/FOR-CAST/anthroDisturbance_Generator>

  - <https://github.com/FOR-CAST/potentialResourcesYT_DataPrep>
